Now here’s the mechanic that people get wrong constantly, and if you learn one thing from this video, learn this one.

When you trade a player, the signing bonus does not travel with him.

Tennessee paid that six million up front in 2024. It’s theirs. It stays on their books. Tennessee eats two million dollars in dead money the moment they trade him — and here’s the detail that makes a midseason move realistic: that two million is the same whether the trade happens before or after June first. There’s no deadline trick. The number doesn’t change.
