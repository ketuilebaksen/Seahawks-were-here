Twelves, that's exactly right and let me make sure you have it. Personnel groupings are two digits: the first is running backs, the second is tight ends. So "thirteen personnel" is ONE back and THREE tight ends — which leaves one receiver.

It's a heavy, physical grouping. And its whole value is DECEPTION: it LOOKS like a run formation, which makes the defense bring in bigger, slower players — and then you throw it to a tight end who can actually run.

Same play. Same call. Same coach. Completely different result.
