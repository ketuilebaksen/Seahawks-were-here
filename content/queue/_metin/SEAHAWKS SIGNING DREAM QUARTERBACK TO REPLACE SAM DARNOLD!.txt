And there's a rule wrinkle here that a lot of fans don't know about, so let me explain it, because it changes the math completely.

The NFL has an emergency third quarterback rule. If you designate a third quarterback on your game-day roster, he can enter the game if both of your other quarterbacks go down — without costing you a roster spot the way it used to. But here's the catch: that emergency quarterback has to be on your 53-man roster or elevated from the practice squad before the game. You cannot conjure one out of thin air in the second quarter when your starter's helmet comes off.

So Seattle's choice isn't "do we want a third quarterback." It's "do we want to have an answer ready, or do we want to find out the hard way on a Sunday afternoon in Arizona." Framed like that, it's not really a choice at all.
